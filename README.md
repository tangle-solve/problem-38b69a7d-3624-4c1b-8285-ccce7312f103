### ALGO-000230: Exponential Time Hypothesis
- **Subfield:** Fine-grained and exact exponential complexity
- **Open:** Prove or refute that 3-SAT on \(n\) variables cannot be solved in \(2^{o(n)}\) time.
- **Status:** Formulated around 1999–2001. Exact algorithms improve exponential bases, but no subexponential algorithm or unconditional exponential lower bound is known.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Supports quantitative lower bounds far more precise than NP-hardness.
- **Key works:** Impagliazzo, Paturi, and Zane (2001), *Which Problems Have Strongly Exponential Complexity?*, DOI: 10.1006/jcss.2001.1774.
- **Related:** Statistical physics—algorithmic complexity of constraint-satisfaction landscapes.
- **Aliases:** ETH.
